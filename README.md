# Slynn ERP - Staff Operations Guide

Welcome to the Slynn Admin Panel! This system is designed to be as simple as chatting with an assistant. Here is a quick guide to help you manage your inventory, log sales, and handle daily operations.

## 1. The Assistant (Your Main Hub)
At the top of the screen, you will see a button that says **"✦ What could I help you with today?"**. 
Clicking this opens the **Chatbot Assistant**. This is where you will do almost all of your work.

From the Assistant menu, you can choose to:
* **Log a Sale / Consignment:** Record items you are selling to a customer or shipping to a retailer.
* **Add a New Product:** Add completely new clothing designs to your system.
* **View Product Catalog:** See everything you currently have in stock.
* **View Financials & Orders:** Review past sales and generate invoices.

---

## 2. How to Log a Sale or Consignment
When a customer buys something, or you ship a wholesale order, you need to log it so the inventory updates automatically.

1. Open the Assistant and click **Log a Sale / Consignment**.
2. **Select the Channel:** Choose where the sale happened (Online Retail, Offline Retail, or Wholesale).
3. **Enter Customer Details:** Type in the customer's name (and optionally their email, phone, and address).
4. **Add Products to the Cart:**
   * **Select Existing Product:** Click this if the clothing is already in your system. Search for the item (e.g., "Wolf Tee"), click it, and you'll see all available sizes. Click "Add Size +", enter how many you sold and the price, and hit "Confirm".
   * **Add New Product:** Click this if you are selling a brand new item that has never been entered into the system before. Fill out the name, colors, and the sizes/quantities you are logging.
5. **Review Consignment Summary:** Once your cart is ready, click this button to see a final breakdown of the grand total.
6. **Create Consignment:** Click this to finalize! The system will automatically deduct the stock from your inventory.
7. **Print Invoice:** After success, you can immediately click "Print Invoice" to view and print the official bill.

---

## 3. How to Manage Inventory (Product Catalog)
When you scroll down, you will see your **Product Catalog** (or you can click "View Product Catalog" in the Assistant).

* **Viewing Stock:** Each product card shows the item's details and a list of all sizes with their current stock levels.
* **Updating Stock (Manual Override):** If you count your warehouse and notice a discrepancy, simply click the number next to a size. A small box will appear allowing you to type the correct stock number and hit "Save".
* **Syncing Platforms:** You can click the "Sync" buttons to push your latest inventory numbers to platforms like Flipkart and Meesho. (Note: Currently set up as a simulation).

---

## 4. How to View Past Orders & Financials
To check past sales or reprint an invoice:

1. Open the Assistant and click **View Financials & Orders**.
2. A Ledger table will appear at the bottom of the screen.
3. You can use the **Search Bar** to look up a specific customer name, invoice number, or product name.
4. You can use the **Filter** dropdown to only show sales from "Online Retail" or "Wholesale".
5. To print an invoice from a past order, just click the **"View / Print"** button next to that order.

## Need Help?
If you ever get stuck, just click the **"✕"** button at the top right of the Assistant to close it and start over. The system is designed to prevent accidental data deletion, so feel free to explore!
